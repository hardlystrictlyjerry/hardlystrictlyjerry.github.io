The [Posts Index Page](/posts) reveals that HSJ became 10 years old this summer, and we celebrated this momentous occasion by playing Honolulu Dead Night.  

To add some novelty to the evening, we distributed "Dead Head New Friend" game cards to everyone in the audience:

{% include image-full.html image="2026-09-25-game.jpg" %}

In retrospect, the font should have been way bigger, but people told us they enjoyed the game. We gave out HSJ stickers to everyone who completed their game card during half time.

Here is a post-show photo of some of the folks who attended:

{% include image-full.html image="2026-09-25-oahu-deadheads.jpg" %}

One of our friends texted me this:

*Thanks again for such an epic night of music last night! I'm so proud of you guys and so stoked to hear you all playing so well together and lighting up the crowd like you do!!!*

Another friend commented to Joanne when we started the William Tell Bridge during St. Stephen:

*"I've never heard **any** band play this part before."*


First Set: Samson and Delilah, Alabama Getaway, Jack Straw, Don't Let Go, Mississippi Half-Step, St. Stephen, The Eleven, Franklin's Tower, Stella Blue, Sugar Magnolia

Second Set: Cold Rain and Snow, Deal, Shakedown Street, Scarlet Begonias, Fire on the Mountain, Good Lovin/La Bamba, Playin in the Band, Not Fade Away, GDTRFB

Encore: US Blues
