Robin and Stu Nixon moved from the Bay Area to Kailua and wanted to throw a housewarming party featuring live Grateful Dead music. (Stu saw his first show in 1969, Robin a few years later). Naturally, we were happy to oblige.

First Set: How Sweet It Is, Jack Straw, Don't Let Go, Mississippi Half-Step, Brown-eyed Woman, St. Stephen, The Eleven, Stella Blue, Good Lovin'/La Bamba, 

Second Set: Shakedown, Deal, Midnight Moonlight, Playin in the Band, Uncle John's Band, Days Between, GDTRFB

Encore: Sugar Magnolia, Ripple
